# Style Constraints

## Hard Constraints

- Keep the asset within `8.8s` and readable without sound.
- Keep the asset on one continuous stage with two half-screen phone frames from the first shot.
- Keep the proof chain fixed:
  - Lucius answers naturally
  - the user mistakes Lucius for a human teammate
  - the reveal lands
  - the sequence closes on `0 handoff requests`
- The root preview must autoplay as a social animation, not as a static composition.
- Every screen must still keep one primary motion only.

## Allowed Optimizations

- Use type-on message animation inside the phones.
- Use a thinking / typing state in the composers before message sends.
- Let the right phone switch from manual typing to automated `Thinking...` before `Wow.` appears.
- Use a squeeze transition where the right phone exits and the left phone takes center.
- Use a restrained logo zoom for the final resolve.

## Prohibited Deviations

- Do not use landing page grammar, app chrome, or dashboard framing.
- Do not rebuild Discord, a full Lucius product page, or a service strip scene.
- Do not use employee ID, tipping, or reward language literally in English copy.
- Do not overdramatize the reveal with neon beams, particle storms, or heavy glows.
- Do not let any message escape the phone screens into a page-like text layout.

## Layout Rules

- One dominant full-bleed stage only.
- Two phones are the only major compositional objects until the final logo resolve.
- Both phones must read as real devices, not cards.
- The phones should be cropped by the frame edges and angled slightly toward the center.
- The `0 handoff requests` proof must appear as a compact in-between chip during the squeeze transition, not as a footer bar.

## Motion Rules

- Screen 1 is right-phone input and send.
- Screen 2 is left-phone thinking and answer generation.
- Screen 3 is right-phone praise message send.
- Screen 4 is left-phone reveal.
- Screen 5 is right-phone `Thinking...` to `Wow.` plus the squeeze transition and proof chip.
- Screen 6 is the centered Lucius device resolving into the logo and final proof sentence.
- Ambient motion may drift, but only one proof action can dominate each screen.

## Type Rules

- Use `SF Pro` or a close system fallback for all interface text and messages.
- Use `Recoleta` only for the final proof sentence or one restrained resolving line.
- Do not use oversized headline typography during the dialogue screens.

## Color Rules

- Use `Warm Ivory` as the field.
- Use `Lucius Purple` as the main Lucius anchor.
- Use `Lavender Mist` and `Orchid Bloom` only as restrained atmosphere.
- Keep the user phone slightly cooler and more neutral than the Lucius phone.
