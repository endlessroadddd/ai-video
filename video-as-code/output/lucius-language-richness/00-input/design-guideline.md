# Design Guideline

Use the Lucius brand system as the governing visual reference, but stage this asset as a dual-device motion piece instead of a page.

Specific guidance for this asset:

- first read should be `two phones in conversation`, not website and not app UI
- keep one full-bleed stage with two half-screen devices as the only major objects
- let the phones carry the story through choreography rather than through page sections
- keep Lucius branding subtle at first and stronger only after the reveal
- make the final shift from two phones to one centered Lucius phone the memorable motion signature
- end on a Lucius logo resolve plus a short proof sentence, not on a page state
- motion should feel deliberate, cinematic, and product-confident rather than graphic-design flashy

Explicit non-goals:

- no landing-page grammar
- no dashboard chrome
- no full-screen chat page
- no service strip composition
- no footer proof ribbon
- no sci-fi glow spectacle
