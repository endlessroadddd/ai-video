# Product Context

Lucius should read as an AI teammate inside customer support and community work, not as a detached chatbot and not as a generic AI brand moment.

For this short asset, the product context baseline is:

- the core value is human-feeling service language in a real support thread
- Lucius should sound like someone on the team who knows the product and why it exists
- the strongest proof is that the answer feels natural enough that no one asks for a human handoff
- the support context should stay legible even when the final surface becomes a motion-led branded stage
- the motion should amplify credibility, not replace it

Positioning anchors:

- Lucius is a teammate in the workflow
- Lucius should feel warm, specific, and competent
- Lucius should not read like a canned support bot
- Lucius should not read like a static campaign poster with AI copy on top

Guardrails:

- do not build the story around deception or AI reveal
- do not overclaim replacement of every human touchpoint
- do not use flashy AI magic as a substitute for product proof
- keep the proof chain limited to natural language plus zero handoff requests
