# DubbingAI Core Brief

## Audience
- Founders, product teams, and community operators who already run or are about to run a product community.
- Buyers who understand Discord as a support surface, not just a growth channel.

## Why
- Large Discord communities break small teams when support volume scales faster than people can respond.
- The visible problem is not member count. It is repeated questions, delayed answers, escalation burden, and humans staying on-call all the time.

## How
- Lucius learns from historical Discord conversations, repeated questions, and prior human resolution patterns.
- It keeps improving through live interactions and escalation feedback instead of freezing after setup.

## What
- Lucius becomes an autonomous Discord support teammate that handles most repetitive support work, keeps coverage running, and escalates edge cases when needed.

## Highlight
- DubbingAI community scale: `30K members`
- Result: `80% automation in 15 days`
- Operating-model shift: `3 -> 0 human mods`
- Coverage: `24/7`
- Human payoff: `Lucius basically gave me my holidays back.`

## Public Proof Points To Preserve
- 30K members
- 80% automation
- 15 days
- 3 -> 0 human mods
- 24/7 coverage
- holiday-relief testimonial

## Must-Keep Product Claims
- Lucius learns from actual community behavior.
- Lucius handles repetitive community support work.
- Lucius escalates what still needs a human.
- Lucius changes the operating model, not only response speed.

## Qualified Claims
- We can infer repeated questions and operational overload from the public case framing, but we should not invent internal queue counts or response-time metrics.
- We can use the Lucius community essay as worldview and interpretation, not as literal DubbingAI testimony.

## Brand Worldview Role
- Good communities are about participation, not just reach.
- The room has to feel alive.
- Structure should stay minimal.
- Support should help the community sustain itself.

## Narrative Guardrails
- Customer case leads.
- English on-screen copy only.
- Brand-narrative plus explainer form.
- Ending lands on method and principle, not CTA.
