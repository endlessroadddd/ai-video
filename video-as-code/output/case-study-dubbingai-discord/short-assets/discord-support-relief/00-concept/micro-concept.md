# Micro Concept

## Asset Goal

Deliver a short proof-style asset that shows Lucius taking repetitive Discord support load off the human team.

## Sequence Idea

- Start inside a busy Discord support surface where repetitive questions keep stacking.
- Shift to Lucius taking over the repetitive response layer with calm, product-aware replies.
- Land on the operating-model proof that the team moved from `3` human moderators to `0`, plus the holiday-relief quote.

## Guardrails

- Keep the asset inside one clear idea.
- Stay silent-first and readable in one pass.
- Do not turn it into a full customer story with multiple subplots.
