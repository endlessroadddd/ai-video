# Discord Support Relief Asset Brief

## Single Takeaway

Lucius takes repetitive Discord support off the human team.

## Tension

- Large Discord communities overload small teams with repetitive support questions.
- The pain is not only response speed; it is humans staying on-call all the time.

## Proof

- `30K members`
- `80% automation in 15 days`
- `3 -> 0 human mods`
- `Lucius basically gave me my holidays back.`

## Audience

- Founders
- Product teams
- Community operators
