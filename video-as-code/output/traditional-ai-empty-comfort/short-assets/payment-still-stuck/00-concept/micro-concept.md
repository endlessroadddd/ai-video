# Micro Concept

## Asset Goal

Turn the pain of empty AI reassurance into a short-form proof asset from the user's point of view.

## Sequence Idea

- Start with the user's payment complaint in a community support surface.
- Show the assistant replying with polished but useless reassurance.
- Show the user pushing for a real answer and getting a fake "resolved" reply.
- Cut back to the product payment UI where the flow is still blocked.

## Guardrails

- Keep the sequence sharp, tense, and realistic.
- Make the money-related failure visually obvious.
- Do not drift into joke framing or over-acted parody.
