# Style Constraints

## Hard Constraints

- Keep the asset within `6-10s` and optimize for silent-first readability.
- The failing payment state must be the most visually credible proof shot in the sequence.
- The generic AI assistant surface must not resemble Lucius branding or Lucius UI language.
- Use Lucius brand discipline for composition quality and motion restraint, but do not make the broken assistant feel like Lucius.
- No flashy AI magic, neon beams, or over-the-top glitch effects.

## Allowed Optimizations

- Use contrast and cropping to make money-related failure text more immediate.
- Compress chat density to increase pressure in the first two beats.
- Use subtle emphasis on "3 days", "payment failed", and "still stuck".

## Prohibited Deviations

- Do not make the sequence comedic or meme-like.
- Do not let soft comfort copy overpower the actual payment failure.
- Do not add multiple competing takeaways.
- Do not imply Lucius is the bad assistant in the negative example.

## Layout Rules

- One primary reading path per beat.
- In chat beats, keep the user's complaint and the assistant's empty comfort visually separable.
- In the proof beat, product UI state must dominate over chat UI.

## Motion Rules

- Motion should feel readable and slightly uncomfortable, not chaotic.
- Message appearance should support tension, not spectacle.
- The final product-state beat should feel like a dead stop.

## Type Rules

- Use sans-serif throughout the negative example.
- If a closing Lucius tag appears later in implementation, keep it restrained.

## Color Rules

- Use neutral or off-brand support-surface colors for the generic assistant.
- Reserve stronger contrast for payment failure text and blocked-action states.
- Avoid letting Lucius Purple dominate the broken-assistant UI.
