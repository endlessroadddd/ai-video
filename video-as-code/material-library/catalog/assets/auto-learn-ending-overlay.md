---
Auto Learn Ending Overlay is the reflective close for this project family. It works when the sequence has already shown enough process and evidence, and the final move is to distill that into one calm statement.

For matching, think about beats like "end on the idea" or "close on one sentence after the proof." It is not the right choice for a metric-heavy result card or for a transition that still needs to explain what happened.

For adaptation, rewrite the line first and keep the one-sentence cadence intact. The centered italic statement and the warm matte are the identity of the asset. Once it becomes dense copy, it stops reading like a proper ending overlay.
