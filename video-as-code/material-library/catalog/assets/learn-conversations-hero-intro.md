Learn Conversations Hero Intro is the right opening when the story begins with scattered signals becoming something structured and actionable. It preserves the original learn-from-conversations hero card and its pacing, so the beat feels like synthesis and product reasoning, not like a generic title slate.

For matching, look for phrases like "signal to knowledge", "feedback synthesis", or "we need to show many conversations becoming one clear pattern." It is strongest when the very next shot will unpack grouped proof, citations, or extracted conclusions. If the message is really about one command propagating across surfaces, prefer One Command Hero Intro instead.

For adaptation, rewrite the lead line, accent word, subline, and kicker first. Keep the centered hero-card composition and the knowledge emphasis intact. Once the intro starts carrying too many proof objects or concrete UI elements, it stops feeling like the original extracted beat and should become a different asset.
