# Final Sequence QA Rules

These rules apply to the heavy lane.
Short assets use `rules/quality/asset-qa-rules.md`.

The full sequence review happens only after all scenes have passed scene QA.

## Sequence integrity

- Scene order matches the approved storyboard.
- Transitions preserve context and do not feel abrupt unless intentionally specified.
- Pacing across scenes feels coherent and cumulative.

## Visual consistency

- The design system stays coherent across the entire sequence.
- Shared motifs, spacing, and motion language remain stable.
- There are no isolated scenes that feel like a different product or visual family.
- The full sequence remains consistent with `rules/design/lucius-brand-application-rules.md` and `rules/design/lucius-brand-qa-checklist.md` when Lucius branding is in scope.
- Logo treatment, type roles, palette hierarchy, and graphic language remain stable from scene to scene.

## Technical quality

- No flicker across transitions.
- No broken layering, clipping, or layout shifts in the final sequence.
- Exported previews and renders match the intended composition.

## Storyboard fidelity

- Overall narrative and scene takeaways still match the approved storyboard.
- End-to-end flow preserves the intended meaning from the core brief.

## Brand fidelity

- The sequence still feels intelligent, collaborative, and quietly confident at the end-to-end level.
- Functional color mappings and Recoleta headline moments remain intentional instead of arbitrary.
- Halftone or dot-grid treatments support the sequence without overwhelming it.
