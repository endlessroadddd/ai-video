# Lucius Brand QA Checklist

Use this checklist during scene review and final-sequence review.

## A. Brand personality

- Does the output feel intelligent rather than gimmicky?
- Does it feel collaborative rather than robotic or domineering?
- Does it feel quietly confident rather than loud or self-congratulatory?

## B. Logo usage

- If the logo appears, is it one of the approved color variants?
- Is logo contrast strong enough for immediate legibility?
- Is minimum clear space preserved?
- Has the logo avoided shadows, outlines, distortion, low resolution, and low-contrast placement?

## C. Color system

- Are all major brand colors inside the approved palette?
- Does the scene use Lucius Purple as an anchor rather than just another accent?
- Is Warm Ivory used as a grounding neutral where appropriate?
- If functional areas are color-coded, do they follow the preferred semantic mapping or have a documented reason not to?
- Are accent colors restrained enough that the palette still feels premium and coherent?

## D. Typography

- Is SF Pro carrying UI, body, and system communication?
- Is Recoleta limited to headlines, brand moments, or key messaging?
- Is Recoleta absent from dense UI and small functional labels?
- Does the typography pairing feel like structure plus warmth, not font clutter?

## E. Visual language

- Are forms simplified, geometric, and softly rounded?
- Are icon shapes clean, modular, and consistent in stroke behavior?
- Does the graphic language feel like Lucius rather than a generic SaaS kit?

## F. Halftone and graphics

- If halftone imagery is used, does it bridge human imagery and digital abstraction?
- Does the dotted matrix support the composition instead of overpowering it?
- Are background grids or dot fields subtle enough to preserve readability?

## G. Motion and storytelling

- Does motion preserve quiet confidence?
- Are brand moments calm, readable, and intentional?
- Is the output free from flashy AI-magic effects that conflict with the brand system?
- If the work is a sequence, do brand choices stay coherent from scene to scene?
